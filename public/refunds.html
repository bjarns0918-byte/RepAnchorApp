Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

We want this to be simple and fair. Here is exactly how trials, refunds, and cancellation work at RepAnchor.

Free trial

Every new account starts with a 7-day free trial. You are charged automatically at the end of the trial, based on the plan you chose ($35/month or $395/year). If you cancel before the trial ends, you won't be charged at all.

Refunds: 24 hours

If you're not satisfied, you can request a full refund within 24 hours of being charged. This applies to both the monthly and annual plans.

It's automatic. For 24 hours after a charge, a "Request a refund" button appears in Settings → Billing. Click it, confirm your password, and your latest payment is refunded in full right away and your subscription ends. No emails and no explanation needed. If the button isn't showing and you think it should, email repanchorapp@gmail.com and I'll sort it out.

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.
  • Requests made more than 24 hours after a charge are not refunded, except where the law requires it.
  • If you request a refund, your subscription ends and your account is closed.

Cancel anytime

You can cancel at any time, with no cancellation fees and no contracts.

  • Cancel yourself from Settings in your account. We email a 6-digit code to confirm it's really you.
  • After you cancel, you keep access through the end of the billing period you've already paid for.
  • You won't be charged again after that.
  • Canceling stops future charges. It doesn't refund a period you've already paid for, unless you're within the 24-hour refund window above.

Deleting your account

You can also permanently delete your account from Settings. This cancels billing immediately and removes your data, as described in our Privacy Policy. Deleting an account does not by itself trigger a refund.

Questions

Email repanchorapp@gmail.com and I'll respond personally. See also the Terms of Service.
