Getting reviews that stick

  • Ask in person, right after a great moment: when someone compliments the food or says thanks.
  • Put the sign by the register, on tables, or tucked in the check presenter.
  • Print the QR on receipts, takeout bags, or menus.
  • Ask everyone, not just happy customers. Google's rules don't allow offering a discount or gift in exchange for a review, or only asking people you expect to be positive.
